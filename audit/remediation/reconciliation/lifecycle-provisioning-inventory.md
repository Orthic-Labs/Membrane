## Required refusal and stable-current rules

The implementation must retain `Refuse(OfflineKnown)`, `Refuse(Denied)`, `Refuse(CorruptOrRotation)`, and `Refuse(TimeoutKnown)` exactly; only `NotFound` may select `ProvisionPackaged` (`engine/crates/membrane-client/src/binding.rs:100-110`). Known incompatible installations must route to canonical update/repair, not a second installation. Runtime executable, assets, state, and lifecycle calls must remain under verified installer-owned `current`; development roots, CWD, PATH, staging, and copied binaries are not authority (`docs/architecture/execution-lifecycle-boundary.md`, “Runtime lifecycle binding”; `docs/architecture/integrations/coderight.md`, §1.1).
