## Acceptance interpretation

The qualification rows above are requirements, not successful runs. A named `RELEASED` acceptance boundary must include the shipped binary, host/version, actual entry point, tool/source class, observed request/response, renderer and resolver. Helper-level success does not establish installed-path delivery.

PSH-004's historical local-transform result does not validate host token accounting. PSH-012's native refusal/forwarding is retained; it does not repair unsupported compatibility clients. PSH-022's parser does not establish store availability, authorization or expiry. Those independent obligations remain open under their owning atoms.

For PSH-014, source-backed spans must be validated against an independent immutable original, while generated elision markers are typed metadata and reversible codecs use their declared inverse/equivalence. Protected obligations are assessed before omission, not inferred from the surviving output. Extractiveness alone does not prove preservation of negation, relationships or required context.

PSH-024 remains open until all its committed line/index/field/key semantics are qualified. A byte/line-only first increment must be marked PARTIAL, not DELIVERED. Full-restore fallback is permitted only when authorized and bounded; otherwise return a typed miss/limit result. Semantic-value projections must not be labeled exact original-byte slices.

PSH-025 is consumer/tool-discovery and store-binding eligibility for Push artifacts; PUL-035 remains the planner's immediate pre-publication resolver recheck. PSH-027 is a subordinate optional-optimization gate, not a new relevance planner or replacement for PSH-011 capacity selection. Capacity-mandated capping can proceed only under its safety/recovery contract and must not be mislabeled as measured cost savings.

PSH-028 adds an explicit retention promise and visible state; PSH-023 remains the read-time expiry refusal. Revocation or an authorized purge may terminate retention explicitly. PSH-029 applies per-artifact resource limits under inherited Membrane scheduling/cancellation; it creates no independent scheduler.
