# Support & commercial boundaries

Membrane is local-first infrastructure. The free/local baseline covers
repository-confined context, local persistence, authority and freshness
decisions, typed receipts, local update verification/rollback paths, and export. These remain
usable without an account, payment, hosted service, or telemetry.

There is no published paid plan, price, SLA, or support response target. Their
availability is **unavailable**. Team sync, fleet controls, central policy,
managed updates, and optional telemetry are **undecided**, not commitments.
This page does not grant a license or promise public availability.

If those services are ever offered, they may add coordination or convenience
only. They must never gate local safety, authority, receipts, updates, or
export. Local verification and user control remain canonical.

For current operational support, see [`policy.md`](policy.md).
